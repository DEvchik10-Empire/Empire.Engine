## Stylized Sky (GDQuest)

`Common/Sky.hlsli`, `Sky.vs.hlsl`, `Sky.ps.hlsl` are an HLSL port of the Godot shader
`stylized_sky.gdshader` from https://github.com/gdquest-demos/godot-4-stylized-sky
(MIT, source code). The preset values in `Tests/Rendering/SceneMain.cpp` come from
that project's `day_sky.tres` / `night_sky.tres`.

The project's art asset `sky/assets/shooting_star_sampler.png` (CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0,
(c) GDQuest, non-commercial, share-alike) is embedded as a C array in
`Engine/Rendering/Empire.Texture/ShootingStarImage.cpp`. Use it only in line with
that license (or replace it with `MakeShootingStarImage`). The noise textures are not
shipped by the original project (Godot generates them with FastNoiseLite); here they are
generated by `Empire.Texture/NoiseImage.cpp`.

Voronoi: Inigo Quilez (MIT). Volumetric rendering idea: Alan Zucconi.
